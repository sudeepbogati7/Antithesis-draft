Surfing for Farmers: proving what rural communities already felt.

Surfing for Farmers takes farmers off the land and into the surf to protect their mental health. The impact was visible on every beach — but funders needed more than stories. Antithesis built the SROI framework and impact reporting that turned lived outcomes into measurable, transparent evidence for funding partners.

When we needed to understand and communicate the real impact Surfing for Farmers was having on rural communities, Antithesis delivered. Their impact and SROI reporting gave us genuine insight into our outcomes while providing our funding partners with the measurable, transparent evidence they needed.
