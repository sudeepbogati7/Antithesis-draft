Privacy Policy

Last updated — 10 May 2026

Antithesis Consulting (“Antithesis”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of personal information we collect, hold, use, and disclose in the course of our business. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By using our website at antithesis.co (the “Site”), contacting us, or engaging us for services, you consent to the collection and handling of your personal information as described in this Policy.

1. About us

Antithesis Consulting is a Melbourne-based fractional leadership and impact-measurement consultancy. We are an APP entity for the purposes of the Privacy Act and are accountable for the personal information we handle.

2. The kinds of personal information we collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

Identification & contact details — your name, role, organisation, work email address, phone number, and postal address.

Engagement information — information you share with us when discussing a possible engagement, during the engagement itself, or in onboarding documentation. This may include commercial information, organisational context, and (where relevant to the work) information about your colleagues, board, donors, or stakeholders.

Site interaction data — technical information collected automatically when you visit the Site, including IP address, browser type and version, device information, pages visited, referring URL, and session timing. Where lawfully permitted, this is collected via cookies and similar technologies (see Section 8).

Correspondence — the content of emails, contact-form submissions, calls, and meeting notes you have with us.

We do not actively seek “sensitive information” (as defined under the Privacy Act, e.g. health, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, sexual orientation) and ask that you not provide it unless it is genuinely necessary for the engagement and we have agreed how to handle it.

3. How we collect personal information

We collect personal information directly from you wherever practicable — for example, when you submit the contact form on our Site, email us, attend a meeting, sign an Engagement Letter, or share documents with us during a project.

We may also collect personal information from third-party sources where it is unreasonable or impracticable to collect from you directly — for example, from your colleagues during a project, from publicly available sources (such as company registers or LinkedIn), or from referees and referral partners.

4. Why we collect, hold, use & disclose your information

We use personal information for purposes that are reasonably necessary for, or directly related to, our consulting activities, including:

(a) responding to enquiries and providing information about our services;

(b) scoping, negotiating, performing, and invoicing consulting engagements;

(c) preparing deliverables, including research, frameworks, and impact reports, where these incidentally contain information about your personnel, stakeholders, or beneficiaries;

(d) managing our client relationships, including sending occasional updates relevant to organisations we have worked with;

(e) operating, securing, and improving the Site and our internal systems;

(f) meeting our legal, regulatory, accounting, and tax obligations; and

(g) protecting our legal rights, including in connection with disputes or insurance claims.

We will not use your personal information for direct marketing other than to send you information clearly relevant to a current or recent engagement, and you can opt out of any such communications at any time using the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.

5. To whom we may disclose personal information

We disclose personal information only where it is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in Section 4, and to recipients including:

(a) our personnel, contractors, and associates who require access to perform services;

(b) trusted service providers who help us operate our business — for example, providers of email and document collaboration, cloud storage, accounting, invoicing, analytics, and IT security — who are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations;

(c) your nominated personnel and authorised stakeholders within your organisation, where this is part of the engagement;

(d) our professional advisers (for example legal, accounting, audit, or insurance) where appropriate; and

(e) regulators, courts, or other authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

We do not sell personal information.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers (for example, cloud-based email, document storage, video conferencing, and analytics platforms) may store data on servers located outside Australia, including in the United States, the European Union, and the United Kingdom. Where we disclose personal information to overseas recipients, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles the information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, typically through contractual protections and the recipient’s own privacy framework.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. This includes using encrypted-in-transit communications, access controls on internal systems, secure cloud-storage providers, multi-factor authentication for sensitive systems, and confidentiality obligations on our personnel and contractors.

While we take security seriously, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If you become aware of any security issue affecting your information held by us, please contact us immediately.

8. Cookies & analytics

The Site uses essential cookies necessary for the Site to function and may use analytics cookies (such as those provided by privacy-friendly analytics tools) to understand aggregated, anonymous usage patterns — for example, which pages are most viewed and how visitors arrive at the Site.

You can configure most browsers to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are set. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the Site. We do not use cookies for cross-site tracking or behavioural advertising.

9. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. In practice this means we typically retain engagement-related information for a minimum of seven (7) years from the end of the engagement, in line with Australian record-keeping and tax-law requirements. After that period, information is securely destroyed or de-identified unless we are required to retain it for longer.

10. Your rights: access, correction, & complaints

Subject to limited exceptions permitted by law, you have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading. We will respond to access and correction requests within a reasonable period (typically within thirty (30) days).

If you are concerned about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us in the first instance using the details in Section 12 and we will try to resolve the issue. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

11. Children

Our services are directed to organisations and their personnel, not to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe we have inadvertently received such information, please contact us so we can delete it.
