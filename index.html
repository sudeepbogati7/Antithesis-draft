01 / The Case

The full-time hire is the wrong default.

Most early-stage and not-for-profit organisations hire senior leadership too early or too late. Too early burns runway; too late, the strategic gaps compound into existential problems.

Fractional leadership resolves the timing paradox: senior expertise scoped to the decisions that actually matter — without the fixed cost of a full-time hire.
