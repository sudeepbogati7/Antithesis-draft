01 / The Case

The full-time hire is the wrong default.

Most early-stage companies hire senior leadership too early or too late. Too early, and the overhead burns runway before the role justifies itself. Too late, and strategic gaps compound into existential problems.

Fractional leadership resolves the timing paradox: you get the experience of a seasoned executive, scoped to the decisions that actually matter, without the fixed cost of a full-time hire. It is how smart companies build strategic capacity without overbuilding the org chart.
