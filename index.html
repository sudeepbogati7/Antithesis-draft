04 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a fractional executive?

A fractional executive is a senior leader — a fractional CEO, CFO, CMO, COO or other C-suite operator — who works with your organisation part-time, on retainer or scoped to a project. You get the experience of a seasoned executive without the cost and commitment of a full-time hire. It’s ideal for startups, scale-ups and not-for-profits that need senior leadership but can’t yet justify a permanent role.

How is fractional leadership different from interim or consulting?

Interim leaders fill a full-time seat temporarily; traditional consultants advise from the outside and leave a report. Fractional leaders embed in your team part-time and own the outcomes from inside the room — building genuine capability rather than dependency. It’s a flexible, on-demand model that scales up or down as your needs change.

What is Social Return on Investment (SROI)?

SROI is a framework for measuring and valuing the social, environmental and economic outcomes an organisation creates. It expresses impact in a ratio — the social value generated for every dollar invested — backed by stakeholder engagement and a Theory of Change. It gives funders, boards and impact investors transparent, defensible evidence of the difference you make.

What impact and ESG services do you provide?

We deliver SROI analysis, social impact measurement, Theory of Change, outcomes and impact reporting, ESG and sustainability reporting, social value and social procurement advice, program evaluation and stakeholder engagement — built specifically for not-for-profit organisations, social enterprises and purpose-driven businesses.
